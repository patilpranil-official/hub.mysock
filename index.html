IITB Research Internship Awards 2026–27 Applications closed

IIT Bombay Internship

Official programme: IITB Research Internship Awards 2026–27, administered through IIT Bombay's Industrial Research & Consultancy Centre (IRCC). The programme is designed as a full-time educational and professional research experience on specific research projects under IIT Bombay faculty.

Duration
4–6 months, full-time
Stipend
₹15,000 / month
Accommodation
Sharing basis, subject to availability; for non-Mumbai candidates
Advisor
IIT Bombay faculty only

Important dates — 2026–27

  • Application deadline: 5:30 PM, 29 September 2026 (extended deadline).
  • Shortlisted candidates: 13 October 2026.
  • Interviews: 26 October to 5 November 2026.
  • Selection results: by 10 December 2026.
  • Internship joining: 21 December 2026 to 20 January 2027; exact joining date is discussed with the concerned faculty in-charge.

Eligibility

  • Second year of a Master's programme, or third/fourth year of a Bachelor's programme, subject to project requirements.
  • Among the top 20% in the previous year in the relevant discipline.
  • Must remain a full-time student through completion of the internship.
  • Must be able to work full-time; part-time internship is not allowed.
  • No course-work requirement during the internship period; the required certificate must be submitted.
  • Application cannot be edited after submission. Duplicate applications using multiple email IDs are rejected.

How to apply

  1. Register on the official IRCC internship portal.
  2. Review the available research projects and choose projects matching your background.
  3. Complete the online application.
  4. Submit a 500-word Statement of Purpose covering your research interest, relevant skills/experience, career plan and the role of the IIT Bombay internship.
  5. Upload proof that you are in the top 20% of your discipline in the previous year, in the prescribed certificate format.
  6. Submit the final version — draft applications are not considered for shortlisting.

Selection procedure

Applications are first screened, followed by a test/interview conducted in a decentralized manner at the department level. Interns have only IIT Bombay faculty as advisors, and only one proposal from a faculty member is considered.

Campus accommodation

For the current award page, sharing accommodation is stated as subject to availability and applicable to non-Mumbai candidates. The 2026–27 award page does not publish a guaranteed room reservation or a current accommodation fee, so users should verify the latest hostel information directly with IIT Bombay/IRCC before relying on it.

FAQ highlights

  • IIT Bombay students are not eligible for these awards.
  • MCA and M.Sc. students can apply subject to the other eligibility requirements.
  • Integrated M.Sc. students are eligible subject to the criteria.
  • Applicants who have already completed the relevant degree are not eligible.
  • Students can apply to multiple projects but can be selected for only one.
  • The internship is not a degree programme and does not provide access to IIT Bombay campus placements.
  • Usual institute working hours are stated as 9:15 AM–5:45 PM, Monday to Friday.

Source: official IIT Bombay IRCC Research Internship Awards portal. Information is shown for educational/information purposes; always verify the live official portal for changes.
